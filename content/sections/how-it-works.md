---
title: How It Works
---

## 1. Create a Booking Request

Click the 'Book' button and fill in your flight details (flight number, arrival time, destination, etc.)

## 2. Get Matched

We'll contact you via email or phone once we find a flight buddy. A small 5% service fee applies to confirm your match.

## 3. Share the Ride

After your flight, you and your matched flight buddies complete the journey together. Split the cab fare with your buddies and pay directly to the driver.
